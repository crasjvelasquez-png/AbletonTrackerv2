# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Ableton Live producers checking recorded activity and opening saved projects from the macOS menu bar.

## Product Purpose

Record Ableton activity locally and make current status, recorded time and project shortcuts easy to reach. Tracking correctness and existing user data take priority over visual changes.

## Brand Personality

Calm, clear, polished. The menu-bar refresh follows the user's rounded native reference with a different color palette.

## Anti-references

Decorative glass effects, unrelated system metrics, extra controls and speculative timers. Preserve the compact status item and existing workflows.

## Design Principles

- Show existing source-of-truth values without changing their meaning or formatting.
- Keep common actions immediate and recognizable.
- Use system typography and native interaction conventions.
- Keep presentation separate from tracking and persistence.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain keyboard operation, readable contrast, visible focus and reduced-motion behavior. No additional user-specific requirements are documented.
